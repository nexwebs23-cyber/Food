Royal Shahi Rasmalai

Jump to Recipe
Время45 мин
Порции6 порции
СложностьСредняя
КатегорияDesserts · Pakistan

Rasmalai translates to 'juice of cream' and represents the pinnacle of subcontinent confectionary. Spongy handmade milk cheese patties soak in a silky reduced milk bath infused with green cardamom, Kashmiri saffron strands, and crushed emerald pistachios.

Ингредиенты и способ приготовления

Ингредиенты

  • Full cream milk for rabri 1 liter
  • Dry milk powder (nido or everyday) 1 cup
  • Baking powder 1 level tsp
  • All-purpose flour 1 tbsp
  • Desi ghee melted 1 tbsp
  • Whisked egg 1 medium
  • Sugar 0.75 cup
  • Cardamom pods crushed 4
  • Saffron threads 1 generous pinch
  • Crushed pistachios and slivered almonds 3 tbsp

Способ приготовления

  1. In a wide pot, simmer 1 liter milk with sugar, crushed cardamoms, and saffron on medium heat for 20 minutes until reduced by one-third into a fragrant rabri.
  2. In a bowl, mix milk powder, baking powder, and flour; rub in melted ghee.
  3. Gradually add whisked egg and knead lightly into a soft, slightly sticky dough (do not over-knead).
  4. Immediately shape dough into 12 small smooth crack-free oval patties (they will double in size when cooked).
  5. Gently drop patties into the actively simmering rabri milk one by one.
  6. Cover pot tightly with a lid and cook on medium flame for 10 minutes without opening to allow steam to puff the dumplings.
  7. Remove lid, gently turn each patty with a spoon, and simmer uncovered for 3 more minutes.
  8. Remove from heat, chill thoroughly in refrigerator for at least 4 hours, and garnish with crushed pistachios before serving cold.

Nutrition (approx. per serving)

290 calories · Protein 9 g · Fat 12 g · Carbs 37 g

Совет шефа

Shape and cook the patties immediately after kneading; if the dough sits on the counter, it dries out and causes cracks in the dumplings.
