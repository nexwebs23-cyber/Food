Ambuyat

25 minute · 4 log · Asian Khane · Brunei Darussalam

Shaya hua 4 November 2026

Ambuyat Brunei Darussalam ki national dish hai — sago palm starch ko paani ke saath milaya jata hai aur ubalte hue paani se hilaya jata hai jab tak woh chamkeela, shaffaf, gond jaisa mass na ban jaye. Apne aap mein is ka zaiqa taqreeban nahi hota, is liye ise candas kehlane wali bamboo sticks par lapeta jata hai aur mirch, jheenga paste aur leemo ki tez cacah sauce mein duboya jata hai, aur grilled prawns, machhli aur sabziyon ke saath khaya jata hai. Riwayati tor par ise bina chabaye nigla jata hai, aur kam az kam teen side dishes aur dips ke baghair yeh adhoora hai.

Ajza

  • Sago starch 1 cup (ya tapioca starch substitute ke tor par)
  • Thanda paani 1/2 cup
  • Ubalta hua paani 3 cups
  • Taze laal mirchein 5-7 (cacah ke liye)
  • Bird's eye mirchein 2-3 (optional, extra tez ke liye)
  • Belacan (jheenga paste) 1 tbsp
  • bhuna hua
  • Leemo ka ras 2 tbsp (1-2 leemo se)
  • Shallot 1 chhota
  • Cheeni 1/2 tsp
  • Namak 1/4 tsp

Nutrition (approx. per serving)

138 calories · Protein 1.3 g · Fat 0.2 g · Saturated fat 0 g · Carbs 33.1 g · Sugar 2.9 g · Fiber 1 g · Sodium 220 mg

Banane Ka Tareeqa

  1. 1 cup sago starch ko heatproof bowl mein daalein, 1/2 cup thanda paani milayein aur chamach ya spatula se achhi tarah hilayein jab tak aik hamwaar, ganth se paak paste na ban jaye. Baad mein ganth rokne ke liye yeh step bohat zaroori hai.
  2. Kettle ya bartan mein 3 cups paani ko tez ubal par le aayein.
  3. Ubalta hua paani sago paste mein patli dhaar mein aahista aahista daalein aur lagataar zor se hilate rahein. Mixture foran gaarha hone lagega aur shaffaf ho jayega.
  4. Hilate rahein jab tak poora mixture aik gaarha, chipchipa, gelatin jaisa mass na ban jaye. Yeh zyada tar saaf hona chahiye, halki opalescent chamak ke saath.
  5. Poori tarah badal jane par ambuyat ko 1-2 minute baithne dein. Yeh aur bhi sakht ho jayega. Communal serving bowl mein daal kar garam hi foran serve karein.
  6. Agar aap ka belacan pehle se bhuna hua nahi hai, to 1 tbsp ko khushk pan mein halki aanch par 1-2 minute halke se bhoonein jab tak khushboo na aane lage. Jalane se bachein.
  7. Mirchein aur shallot ko mota mota kaatein. Mortar and pestle mein, ya chhote food processor mein, bhune hue belacan, mirchein, shallot, 1/2 tsp cheeni aur 1/4 tsp namak ko kootain jab tak mota paste na ban jaye.
  8. 2 tbsp leemo ka ras milayein, chakh kar seasoning theek karein, aur cacah ko chhote serving bowl mein daalein. Bamboo candas par thoda ambuyat lapetein, cacah mein duboyein, aur grilled prawns, machhli aur sabziyon ke saath khayein.

Chef ki tip

Starch ko pehle thande paani se hamwaar paste bana kar milayein — yahi woh step hai jo ganth rokta hai. Ubalta hua paani patli dhaar mein daalein aur hilana kabhi na rokein, warna starch chamkeela aur hamwaar banne ke bajaye ganth ban jata hai.

Serve Karna

Garam communal bowl mein serve karein, cacah chhote bowl mein, aur grilled prawns, tali hui machhli, steamed sabziyan aur kheera aur gaajar ki slices aas paas rakhein. Candas par aik chhoti si ball lapetein, duboyein, aur bina chabaye nigal lein — yahi riwayati tareeqa hai.

Store Aur Dobara Garam Karna

Ambuyat taza aur garam hi behtareen khaya jata hai. Thanda hone par yeh sakht aur sookha ho jata hai aur theek se dobara garam nahi hota; utna hi banayein jitna aik dafa mein khayenge. Cacah sauce fridge mein aik din tak rehti hai.

Ajza Ke Notes

  • Ambuyat apne aap mein taqreeban be-zaiqa hota hai — yeh chawal ya noodles jaisa aik neutral starch base hai, aur cacah sauce aur side dishes hi saara zaiqa dete hain.
  • Lapetne se pehle candas ko thode starch wale paani mein duboyein, taake ambuyat sticks par chipke aur phisle nahi.
  • Garam rehte hi foran serve karein. Thanda hone par ambuyat sakht ho jata hai aur apni chamkeeli, khinchne wali texture kho deta hai.
  • Sago starch ki jagah tapioca ya aalu ka starch istemal kiya ja sakta hai, lekin texture asli sago starch se thodi kam elastic hoti hai.

Variations

  • Tempoyak cacah: 2 tbsp fermented durian (tempoyak) ko 1 mirch, 1 tsp jheenga paste aur 1 tbsp sookhe jheenge ke saath kootain, phir thode garam paani se patla karein.
  • Tamarind cacah: 1 cup imli ka ras 2-3 bareek kati lehsan ki kaliyon, 1-2 kati hui mirchein aur aik chutki namak ke saath tab tak ubalein jab tak thoda gaarha na ho jaye.
  • Binjai cacah: leemo, piyaz, lehsan aur binjai phal ko koot kar khatta, masaledaar dip banayein — Brunei ki classic khatti cacah.

Aam Ghaltiyan

  • Thande paani ka paste banana chhod dena. Sookhe starch mein seedha ubalta paani daalne se ganth ban jati hain jo kabhi hamwaar nahi hoti.
  • Ubalta paani bohat tez daalna ya hilana rok dena. Starch jagah jagah jam jata hai aur ambuyat chamkeela hone ke bajaye ganth-daar ho jata hai.
  • Ise thanda serve karna. Thanda ambuyat sakht aur bejaan hota hai; shaffaf hote hi ise garam serve karna chahiye.
  • Ise saade khana. Cacah aur side dishes ke baghair ambuyat taqreeban be-zaiqa hota hai — dips hi asal dish hain.

Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

Ambuyat kis cheez se banta hai?

Ambuyat sago starch se banta hai, sago palm ke tane se nikala gaya safed starch. Starch ko thande paani ke saath mila kar paste banaya jata hai, phir ubalta hua paani hilate hue daala jata hai jab tak woh chamkeela, shaffaf, gond jaisa mass na ban jaye.

Cacah sauce kya hai?

Cacah ambuyat ke saath serve ki jane wali dipping sauce hai. Classic version sambal belacan hai — laal mirchein, bird's eye mirchein, bhuna hua jheenga paste, shallot, cheeni, namak aur leemo ka ras koot kar mota, tez paste banaya jata hai. Doosri cacah mein imli, tempoyak (fermented durian) ya binjai phal istemal hota hai.

Ambuyat kaise khate hain?

Candas kehlane wali bamboo sticks se: sticks ke gird thoda ambuyat ghumayein, cacah sauce mein duboyein, aur bina chabaye nigal lein. Ise grilled prawns, machhli, sabziyon aur doosri side dishes ke saath khaya jata hai.

Kya main sago starch ki jagah tapioca starch istemal kar sakti hoon?

Ji haan. Tapioca ya aalu ka starch aik aam substitute hai aur usi tarah kaam karta hai, halankeh texture asli sago starch se thodi kam elastic hoti hai.
