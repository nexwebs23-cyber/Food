Ilish Bhapa

40 minute · 4 log · Machhli Ke Khane · Bangladesh

Shaya hua 4 November 2026

Ilish Bhapa Bangladesh ki mashhoor steamed hilsa hai — hilsa, Bangladesh ki national machhli, teekhi sarson, dahi aur nariyal ke paste mein hari mirch aur sarson ke tel ke sath steam mein pakayi jati hai. Kachhi machhli ko kabhi tala nahi jata; woh band tiffin box ke andar apni hi bhaap mein halke halke pakti hai, naram, raseeli aur mulayam rehti hai, jab ke sarson ka paste sunehra aur khushboodar ho jata hai. Yeh woh dish hai jo Bengali izzat wale mehmaanon ko serve karte hain, box table par khola jata hai taake bhaap uthti machhli dikhe, aur ise hamesha saade steamed chawal ke sath garam khaya jata hai. Sarson ke beejon ko pakane se pehle do ghante bhigona hota hai, is liye pehle se plan karein — asli pakane mein sirf taqreeban 40 minute lagte hain.

Ajza

  • Hilsa machhli (ilish machh) 250 g
  • 3 cm mote 4 tukde
  • Sarson ke beej 40 g

  • kasa hua

  • phenti hui
  • Namak 22 g (Bong Eats; cross-check recipes mein taqreeban 1 tsp)
  • Cheeni 1/4 tsp
  • Haldi powder 1/2 tsp
  • Sarson ka tel 20 g

Nutrition (approx. per serving)

Banane Ka Tareeqa

  1. Hilsa saaf karein aur 3 cm moti slices mein kaatein, 250 g machhli se taqreeban 4 tukde.
  2. 40 g sarson ke beejon ko 2 ghante paani mein bhigoyein. Chhaanein, phir namak aur 4 hari mirchon ke sath smooth paste banne tak peesein, halke se pulse karein taake sarson karwi na ho.
  3. Kasa hua nariyal, phenti hui dahi, 1/4 tsp cheeni, 1/2 tsp haldi aur 20 g sarson ka tel mila kar gaarha sunehra paste banayein.
  4. Machhli ke tukdon par paste ko har taraf achhi tarah lagayein.
  5. Lep lagi machhli ko tiffin box mein sajayein, baqi 4 hari mirchein aadhi kar ke upar rakhein.
  6. Dhakkan kas kar band karein aur machhli ko kam se kam 15 minute marinate hone ke liye alag rakhein.
  7. Pan mein ek stand rakhein, tiffin box ki aadhi unchai tak garam paani daalein, aur paani ubalne par tiffin ko stand par rakhein. Pan ko dhakein aur medium aanch par 15 minute steam mein pakayein.
  8. Tiffin ko pan se nikaalein aur steamed chawal ke sath garam serve karne se pehle 5 minute aur rest karne dein.

Chef ki tip

Sarson ke beejon ko sirf halke se peesein — zyada peesne se sarson karwi ho jati hai. Do ya teen baar pulse karein, kinaron ko khurachte hue, jab tak bilkul smooth na ho jaye. Aur is dish ke liye machhli ko kabhi talein nahi: paste mein steamed kachhi hilsa naram, raseeli aur mulayam banti hai.

Serve Karna

Tiffin box se seedha garam serve karein, dhakkan table par hi kholein taake khushboodar bhaap sab ke saamne nikle. Har shakhs ko sunehre sarson ke paste ke sath machhli ka ek tukda dein, aur hamesha saade steamed chawal ke sath serve karein — chawal teekha, tel wala paste soakh lete hain aur us ki garmi ko balance karte hain. Sath mein leemo ka tukda optional hai, aur kuch aur nahi chahiye.

Store Aur Dobara Garam Karna

Bachi hui machhli ko band tiffin box mein fridge mein rakhein aur 1 se 2 din mein khayein. Ubalte paani par band box ko taqreeban 5 minute steam mein rakh kar halke se garam karein jab tak poori tarah garam na ho jaye; tez aanch par microwave mein garam na karein ya machhli ko dobara na talein, warna woh sookh jayegi. Ilish Bhapa ko freeze na karein — machhli sookh jati hai aur sarson ke paste ki taaza teekhi khushboo khatam ho jati hai.

Ajza Ke Notes

  • Sarson ke beejon ko poore 2 ghante bhigoyein. Achhi tarah bhigoye beej smooth paste banate hain; sookhe beej danedaar rehte hain aur paste sakht ho jata hai.
  • Bong Eats recipe ki tarah bhoore aur peele sarson ke beej mila kar istemal karein. Bhoore beej teekha zaiqa dete hain, peele beej use halka rakhte hain — sirf bhoori sarson se zaiqa bohat karwa ho sakta hai.
  • Sarson ko sirf halke se peesein. Do ya teen baar pulse karein, kinaron ko khurachte hue, jab tak bilkul smooth na ho jaye — zyada peesna hi bhapa ke karwa hone ki main wajah hai.
  • Is dish ke liye machhli ko kabhi talein nahi. Paste mein steamed kachhi hilsa naram, raseeli aur mulayam banti hai, jab ke tali hui machhli sookh jati hai aur sarson ka zaiqa pheeka par jata hai.

Variations

  • Banana-leaf bhapa: tiffin box ki jagah lep lagi machhli ko kele ke patton mein lapetein aur 30 minute steam mein pakayein, traditional Oh! Calcutta tareeqe se — patta halki khushboo deta hai.
  • Poppy-seed bhapa: zyada rich, mewe-daar paste ke liye sarson ke sath 1.5 tbsp khashkhash peesein, jaisa Debjani ke version mein hai.
  • No-coconut bhapa: nariyal chhod dein aur us ki jagah thodi aur dahi daalein, taake zaiqa zyada teekha aur poori tarah sarson wala ho.

Aam Ghaltiyan

  • Sarson ko zyada dair peesna. Zyada peesi sarson karwi ho jati hai aur poora dish kharab kar deti hai — halke se pulse karein, do ya teen baar, jab tak bilkul smooth na ho jaye.
  • Machhli ko pehle talna. Bhapa ka matlab hai steam mein paka: tali hui hilsa sookh jati hai aur is dish ki mashhoor nazuk, raseeli texture kho jati hai.
  • 2 ghante bhigona chhod dena. Bina bhigoye sarson ke beej danedaar, sakht paste banate hain, smooth sunehra paste nahi.
  • Zyada dair steam mein pakana ya dhakkan dheela rakhna. Medium aanch par 15 minute se zyada pakane se hilsa tootne lagti hai, aur dhakkan kas kar band na ho to bhaap — aur zaiqa — nikal jata hai.

Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

Recipe mein 22 g namak likha hai — kya yeh zyada hai?

Ji haan, yeh thoda zyada hai. Bong Eats mein 22 g likha hai, lekin cross-check recipes mein taqreeban 1 tsp namak istemal hota hai. Agar aap ko yaqeen nahi, to 1 tsp se shuru karein, machhli par lagane se pehle paste chakhain, aur zaroorat ho tabhi aur daalein.

Mera sarson ka paste karwa kyun ho gaya?

Sarson ko zyada dair peesa gaya, ya beej kaafi dair tak nahi bhigoye gaye. Beejon ko poore 2 ghante bhigoyein aur halke se peesein — do ya teen baar, kinaron ko khurachte hue — jab tak bilkul smooth na ho jaye.

Kya Ilish Bhapa banane ke liye tiffin box zaroori hai?

Kas kar band hone wale dhakkan wala steel ka tiffin box classic bartan hai, lekin koi bhi dhakkan wala steel ka dabba jo achhi tarah band ho, kaam karega. Traditional tareeqe se machhli ko kele ke patte mein lapet kar bhi steam mein pakaya jata hai, jis se halki khushboo milti hai.

Kya main Ilish Bhapa pehle se bana sakti hoon?

Aap machhli par paste laga kar tiffin box mein kuch ghante pehle marinate karke fridge mein rakh sakti hain, lekin machhli naram rahe is liye serve karne se theek pehle steam mein pakayein. Bacha hua khana fridge mein 1 se 2 din rehta hai; taqreeban 5 minute halke se steam mein garam karein.
