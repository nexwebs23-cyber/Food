About Us

Zaika Worlds is a home cooking collection for people who want to cook a familiar dish well, and try a new one without guesswork. Every recipe is written to be read in the kitchen, with the dish in front of you before you start.

What Zaika Worlds is, and who it is for

Zaika Worlds gathers home cooking from Pakistan, India and 14 more countries in one place, so a weekday dinner and a dish for guests can both start from the same page. It is for home cooks, for people learning to cook, and for families who want a meal everyone at the table will enjoy.

For everyday cooks

You do not need a long list of tools or hard to find ingredients to begin. Choose a dish, read the method once, and cook at your own pace. The page is built for the kitchen, not for reading about food from a distance.

For cooks who like to plan

Cooking time and servings are shown before you open a recipe, so you can match a dish to the time you have and the people you are feeding. A quick daal on a busy evening and a slow nihari for the weekend sit side by side, and both say plainly how long they take.

How the recipes are written

Every recipe lists clear ingredients first, then the method in steps that follow the order of cooking. Each recipe also has variations to change, common mistakes to avoid, a chef tip that saves time or improves the result, and answers to frequently asked questions.

  • Clear ingredients, with quantities given in the recipe
  • Steps in cooking order, so you know what to do first
  • Variations, for a different main ingredient or a milder dish
  • Common mistakes, so a small error does not spoil the meal
  • A chef tip in every recipe, for the part that matters most
  • Frequently asked questions, answered in plain words

Read the method first

Read the whole method before you start, and keep the ingredients ready. If a method asks for a rest, a soak or a slow cook, that time is part of the recipe, and it is written where you can see it before you begin.

The food in focus

The heart of the collection is desi cooking from Pakistan and India, the dishes many readers grew up eating and still cook for family meals. Around that heart sits world food from 14 more countries, chosen because the dishes travel well into a home kitchen.

Desi dishes from Pakistan and India

Desi Dishes holds biryani, nihari, karahi and daal. Kebabs sit in BBQ, sweets in Desserts and drinks in Drinks, so each group has its own place. There is no bread recipe on the site yet. The methods keep the familiar flavours, and explain the step that makes each dish work.

World dishes from 14 more countries

The world dishes range from pizza in Italy and ramen in Japan to tacos in Mexico and paella in Spain. Each one is written the same way, with ingredients first and steps in order, so a new cuisine feels as clear as a familiar one.

Why it is easy for beginners and families

A first cook needs to know what to buy, what to do first, and when the dish is ready. A family cook needs to know how long dinner takes and how many people it feeds. Every recipe answers those questions before the cooking starts.

  • Quantities and times are given in each recipe, so there is little to guess
  • Steps follow the order of cooking, with the tricky part explained
  • Servings help you plan a family meal, and leftovers are covered in storage notes

What comes next

New dishes will be added gradually, as the collection grows. The aim is steady growth, with each new recipe written to the same pattern, rather than a large number of thin pages.

New countries, added step by step

Dishes from new countries will join the collection one at a time, from the recipe database, with the same big photo, ingredients, method, time and servings. When a new dish is ready, it will appear in the recipe list and in the language menu, like the recipes here now.
