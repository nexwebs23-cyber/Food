Mutton Yakhni Pulao

Temps75 min
Portions5 personnes
DifficultéMoyenne
CatégoriePlats desi · Pakistan

Publié le 5 November 2026

Mutton Yakhni Pulao is the pride of celebration dining across Pakistan and North India. Unlike intensely spiced biryani, authentic pulao derives its delicate yet profound flavour directly from the slow-extracted yakhni broth, infused with fennel seeds, coriander, ginger, garlic, and whole warm spices.

Ingrédients et méthode

Ingrédients

  • Bone-in mutton (shoulder or leg) 800 g
  • Aged basmati rice 3 cups
  • washed and soaked 30 mins
  • Fennel seeds (saunf) 2 tbsp
  • whole coriander seeds 2 tbsp
  • Onions 3 medium
  • thinly sliced
  • Ginger-garlic paste 2 tbsp
  • sliced ginger 1 tbsp
  • Whole spices: 2 bay leaves
  • 4 green cardamoms
  • 1 black cardamom
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 6 cloves
  • Plain yoghurt 1/2 cup
  • whisked
  • Ghee or cooking oil 1/2 cup
  • Salt to taste
  • green chillies 4 slit

Méthode

  1. Prepare the yakhni: In a deep stockpot, combine mutton pieces, 7 cups water, fennel seeds, coriander seeds, sliced ginger, garlic cloves, 1 sliced onion, and salt. Bring to a boil, skim the surface, cover and simmer on low heat for 50-60 minutes until the meat is tender.
  2. Strain the broth through a fine mesh sieve into a bowl, discarding whole spice seeds. Reserve the tender mutton pieces and keep 5 cups of clear strained yakhni broth.
  3. In a wide heavy-bottom pot, heat ghee and fry the remaining sliced onions until golden brown. Remove 1/3 of the onions for garnish.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste, whole spices (bay leaf, cardamoms, cinnamon, cloves), and whisked yoghurt into the pot. Sauté for 3 minutes until oil separates.
  5. Add the boiled mutton pieces and green chillies. Fry gently for 4 minutes to coat the meat in aromatics.
  6. Pour in the 5 cups of strained hot yakhni broth and bring it to a rolling boil. Taste and adjust salt.
  7. Gently add the drained soaked basmati rice. Cook uncovered on medium-high heat until 85% of the liquid is absorbed and small crater holes appear on the surface.
  8. Sprinkle the reserved golden fried onions on top. Seal tightly with foil and a heavy lid, then put on dum over low heat for 15 minutes. Rest 5 minutes before gently fluffing with a fork.

Nutrition (approx. per serving)

680 calories · Protein 36.4 g · Fat 24.1 g · Saturated fat 11.2 g · Carbs 78.5 g · Sugar 3.8 g · Fiber 3.2 g

Conseil du chef

The secret to a royal pulao is tying the fennel and coriander seeds in a cheesecloth bundle (potli) or straining thoroughly so no grit enters the rice. Never stir the rice aggressively once boiling.
