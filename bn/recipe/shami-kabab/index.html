Traditional Beef Shami Kabab

সময়60 মিনিট
পরিবেশন6 জন
কাঠিন্যমাঝারি
বিভাগদেশি খাবার · Pakistan

প্রকাশিত 5 November 2026

Shami Kababs are the quintessential tea-time snack and Eid delicacy across South Asia. Prepared by simmering tender beef cuts with chana daal and whole spices until completely dry, the mixture is pounded to a velvety fibre texture, formed into neat patties, dipped in whisked egg, and fried until golden and crisp.

উপকরণ ও পদ্ধতি

উপকরণ

  • Boneless beef chunks (cut into cubes) 750 g
  • Chana daal (split Bengal gram) 1 cup
  • rinsed and soaked 1 hour
  • Onion 1 large
  • roughly chopped
  • Ginger 2-inch piece chopped
  • garlic 10 whole cloves
  • Whole dry red chillies 8-10
  • cumin seeds 1 tbsp
  • black peppercorns 1 tsp
  • Black cardamom 2
  • cinnamon sticks 2
  • cloves 6
  • Salt 1 1/2 tsp
  • turmeric 1/2 tsp
  • Fresh mint leaves 1/2 cup finely chopped
  • coriander leaves 1/2 cup finely chopped
  • Green chillies 3
  • finely chopped
  • Egg 1 beaten for binding
  • plus 1 egg for dipping during frying
  • Oil for shallow frying

পদ্ধতি

  1. In a pressure cooker or deep pot, combine beef cubes, soaked chana daal, chopped onion, ginger, garlic, whole dry red chillies, cumin, whole spices, turmeric, salt, and 2 1/2 cups water.
  2. Cook until the meat and daal are thoroughly tender and all excess water has completely evaporated. The mixture must be completely dry.
  3. Discard large cinnamon sticks and black cardamom skins. While still warm, crush or grind the meat mixture using a food processor or mortar and pestle until it forms a smooth, stringy, fibrous paste (reshay-dar).
  4. Allow the mixture to cool completely, then mix in chopped mint, fresh coriander, green chillies, and 1 beaten egg.
  5. Grease your palms lightly with oil, take golf-ball sized portions, and shape them into smooth, round flat patties.
  6. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a non-stick frying pan over medium heat.
  7. Dip each kabab gently into whisked egg and shallow fry for 2-3 minutes per side until crisp and golden brown.
  8. Drain on paper towels and serve hot alongside fresh mint raita, onion rings, and lemon wedges.

Nutrition (approx. per serving)

240 calories · Protein 22.8 g · Fat 12.5 g · Saturated fat 4.1 g · Carbs 9.4 g · Sugar 1.1 g · Fiber 2.8 g

শেফের টিপস

Never leave residual moisture in the boiled meat-daal pot; if wet, cook it on high heat stirring constantly until dry, otherwise the kababs will break in the pan.
