Chicken Biryani

60 min · 4 people · Desi Dishes · Pakistan

Published on 28 October 2026

Biryani is the dish most homes save for guests and celebrations, and Karachi style is known for bold spice, separate grains of rice, and the smell of dum that fills the house before the lid comes off. The method below keeps the chicken juicy and the rice fragrant without turning the layers into one soft mass. Marination does the heavy flavour work, so the cooking itself is mostly layering and waiting. Serve it straight from the pot, with raita and salad, and let people take second helpings while it is still steaming.

Ingredients

  • Chicken 750g
  • yoghurt 1 cup
  • Basmati rice 3 cups (soaked)
  • Biryani masala 3 tbsp
  • ginger-garlic paste 2 tbsp
  • Onions 3 (browned)
  • Tomatoes 2
  • green chillies 4
  • Fresh coriander
  • mint
  • lemon
  • Saffron / food colour
  • ghee 3 tbsp
  • Salt
  • bay leaves
  • cardamom
  • cloves (for the rice water)

Method

  1. Wash the rice until the water runs clear, soak it for 30 minutes, and drain. Mix the yoghurt, biryani masala, ginger-garlic paste and salt into the chicken and marinate for 30 minutes.
  2. Boil a large pot of water with salt and the whole spices. Add the rice and cook until it is 70 percent done and still firm in the centre, then drain at once.
  3. Heat the ghee, brown the onions until deep golden, and remove half for layering. Keep the rest in the pan.
  4. Add the tomatoes and the marinated chicken, and fry on medium high heat for 10 to 12 minutes, until the masala thickens and the oil separates.
  5. Spread the chicken masala in a heavy pot. Add half the rice, then sprinkle coriander, mint, brown onion, lemon and saffron.
  6. Add the remaining rice and top with the rest of the onion, herbs and saffron and a spoon of ghee. Seal the lid with a cloth or foil.
  7. Cook on low heat on dum for 20 minutes without opening the lid, then rest for 10 minutes off the heat.
  8. Open the lid, fluff gently from the sides with a fork, mix lightly so the colours stay visible, and serve hot.

Chef's tip

Do not over-boil the rice. It will finish cooking on dum. Put a cloth over the lid on dum so the steam does not escape, and taste the rice water. It should taste slightly salty, because plain rice needs that seasoning.

Serving

Serves 4 generously with raita, kachumber salad and lemon wedges. Put the pot in the centre of the table and open it there, so everyone gets the dum aroma. Naan is optional, because the rice is the main dish. A glass of lassi or a mint drink balances the spice well.

Storage and reheating

Cool within 1 hour and refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 2 days. Reheat in a covered pan on low heat with 2 tbsp of water, or in the microwave covered, until steaming hot throughout. Do not reheat more than once. Freeze in portions for up to 1 month, and thaw in the fridge overnight before reheating.

Ingredient notes

  • Chicken on the bone stays juicier during dum. Use leg pieces and trim away excess fat.
  • Aged basmati gives separate grains. New rice turns soft more easily, so watch the boiling time.
  • Full fat yoghurt makes the marinade cling to the chicken. Low fat yoghurt can split when it hits heat.
  • Biryani masala varies by brand. Taste the masala before adding extra chilli, and adjust next time, not now.

Variations

  • Vegetable biryani: replace the chicken with potatoes, cauliflower, peas and carrots, marinate the vegetables briefly, and shorten the dum to 15 minutes.
  • Mutton biryani: use 750g mutton, cook the masala until the meat is nearly tender before layering, and allow 30 minutes on dum.
  • Less spicy: halve the green chillies, skip extra red chilli, and serve with extra raita.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the rice fully before dum. It turns mushy in the pot.
  • Opening the lid during dum. Steam escapes and the rice stays undercooked.
  • Using too little salt in the rice water. The layers taste flat.
  • Stirring hard after dum. Grains break and the colours mix into one shade.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make biryani without saffron?

Yes. Use a pinch of yellow food colour dissolved in warm milk, or skip it. The flavour comes from the masala and the dum, not from colour alone.

How long should the chicken marinate?

30 minutes is the minimum. Two to four hours in the fridge is better. Keep it refrigerated, and do not leave marinated chicken at room temperature.

Why is my rice sticky?

The rice was overboiled, or it was not drained at once, or the pot was stirred hard. Drain at 70 percent done and rest the pot before fluffing.

Can I use boneless chicken?

Yes. Use thigh pieces and reduce the masala cooking by 3 to 4 minutes, so the chicken does not dry out.
