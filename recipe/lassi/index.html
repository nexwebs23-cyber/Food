Mango Lassi

15 min · 3 people · Drinks · India

Mango lassi is summer in a glass, ripe mangoes blended with cold yoghurt, a little milk, and cardamom, until it is thick, smooth and cold enough to cool you down. It takes 15 minutes, most of which is peeling the mango, and it needs no cooking at all. The quality of the mango decides everything, so choose fruit that smells sweet at the stem, gives slightly when pressed, and has no green, hard patches. Serve it at once, in chilled glasses, with a little chopped mango on top, and drink it while the blender foam is still on top.

Ingredients

  • Ripe mangoes 2, yoghurt 1½ cups
  • Milk 1/2 cup, sugar 2 tbsp (to taste)
  • Cardamom powder 1/4 tsp
  • Ice cubes, mint for garnish

Method

  1. Chill the glasses in the fridge for 15 minutes, so the lassi stays cold while you drink it.
  2. Peel the mangoes, cut the flesh away from the stone, and chop it roughly. Taste a piece and decide how much sugar you need.
  3. Put the mango, yoghurt, milk, sugar and cardamom in a blender. Do not add the ice yet.
  4. Blend for 30 seconds, stop, scrape down the sides, and blend for 30 seconds more, until completely smooth.
  5. Add a handful of ice cubes and blend for 15 to 20 seconds more, just until the ice is crushed and the lassi is thick and cold.
  6. Taste and adjust. Add a little more sugar if the mango is tart, or a splash of milk if it is too thick to pour.
  7. Pour into the chilled glasses at once, leaving the foam on top.
  8. Garnish with chopped mango and a mint leaf, and serve at once with a straw or a spoon.

Tip

The sweeter the mango, the less sugar you need. Do not make lassi in advance. Freshly blended is best, because it separates and loses its foam if it sits, and the ice melts and thins it.

Serving

Serves 3, in tall chilled glasses, with chopped mango and mint on top. Mango lassi suits hot afternoons, spicy meals, and breakfast on a summer weekend. Serve it as a drink with paratha and pickle, or as a light dessert after a spicy curry. Keep it cold until the moment it is poured, and drink it fresh.

Storage

Drink fresh for the best texture. Lassi keeps in a sealed jar in the fridge for up to 1 day, but it separates, so stir or shake well before serving, and do not expect the foam to return. Do not freeze lassi, because the yoghurt turns grainy on thawing. If you must make it ahead, blend the mango and yoghurt without ice, refrigerate, and add ice and blend again just before serving.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use frozen mango?

Yes. Thaw it halfway, so it is soft enough to blend but still cold, and reduce the ice by half. Frozen mango makes a very thick lassi, so add milk a splash at a time.

Is mango lassi healthy?

It is made from fruit and yoghurt, with no numbers added here. The sugar is the part to watch, so use ripe mangoes and add as little sugar as the fruit needs.

Can I make lassi without a blender?

Yes, if the mango is very soft. Mash the mango with a fork, whisk in the yoghurt and milk, and pass it through a sieve. It is less smooth, but still tasty.

Why is my lassi stringy?

The mango was fibrous. Blend longer, or pass the lassi through a fine sieve before serving. Next time, choose a less fibrous mango variety.
