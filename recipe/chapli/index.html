Chapli Kabab

30 min · 4 people · BBQ · Pakistan

Chapli kebab is Peshawar on a plate, flat, soft, richly spiced, and fried on a tawa until the edges turn crisp. The name comes from its flat shape, and the flavour comes from tomato, pomegranate seeds, crushed whole coriander and green chillies mixed right into the mince. It is street food and celebration food at once, eaten with naan, onion and green chutney, and best made with cold mince and gentle hands. Fry it on medium low heat so the centre cooks through without burning the outside, and press a piece of tomato into the middle, as the kebab makers do.

Ingredients

  • Beef mince 500g
  • Tomato 1 finely chopped, onion 1
  • Pomegranate seeds 1 tbsp, crushed whole coriander
  • Green chillies 3, ginger, salt
  • Maize flour 2 tbsp
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Chop the onion and tomato very finely and squeeze out excess tomato juice, so the mix stays firm enough to shape.
  2. Put the mince in a bowl with the tomato, onion, pomegranate seeds, crushed coriander, chillies, ginger, salt and maize flour. Mix with your hands until just combined.
  3. Cover and rest the mix in the fridge for 20 minutes. Cold mince holds its shape and fries better.
  4. Wet your hands with cold water. Take a portion, flatten it into a thin round about 4 inches across, and press a small piece of tomato into the centre.
  5. Heat a generous layer of oil on a tawa on medium heat. The oil should be hot enough to sizzle, but not smoking.
  6. Slide the kebabs in, leaving space between them. Fry for 4 to 5 minutes on the first side without pressing them down.
  7. Turn gently with a wide spatula and fry for 4 to 5 minutes more, until golden on both sides and cooked through.
  8. Lift out, drain briefly, and serve hot with green chutney, sliced onion and naan.

Tip

Do not press the kebabs too much. Turn them gently or they will break. Cold mince makes better kebabs, so keep the mix in the fridge until you shape each batch.

Serving

Serves 4 as a main dish with naan, green chutney, sliced onion, lemon and a salad. Serve 2 kebabs per person with plenty of chutney, and keep extra naan warm. Chapli kebab also works as a starter, one kebab per person with chutney and lemon, before a main course. Mint raita cools the spice nicely.

Storage

Cool and refrigerate cooked kebabs in an airtight container for up to 2 days. Reheat on a tawa on medium heat until hot through, or in the oven at 180C for 8 to 10 minutes. Uncooked mix keeps in the fridge for up to 1 day. Freeze shaped, uncooked kebabs between sheets of baking paper for up to 1 month, and fry from frozen, adding 2 minutes per side.

Common mistakes

Frequently asked questions

Why do my chapli kebabs break?

The mix was too wet, the kebab was too thick, or it was turned too soon. Drain the tomato, chill the mix, keep kebabs thin, and let the first side set before turning.

Can I bake chapli kebabs?

Yes. Brush with oil and bake at 200C for 15 to 18 minutes, turning once. They are less crisp than fried kebabs, but still tasty.

What is pomegranate seed for?

Anar dana gives a sour, fruity note that balances the rich mince. If you cannot find it, use 1 tsp of lemon juice and a few crushed dried cranberries, but the flavour is different.

Can I make the mix ahead?

Yes. Mix and refrigerate for up to 1 day, covered. Shape just before frying, so the kebabs hold their form.
