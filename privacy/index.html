Privacy Policy

This page explains what this site does and does not collect. In short, this site does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect personal data from you. Last updated: October 2026.

No accounts, no logins and no comments

There is no user account, login, comment or password on this site. You do not need to give a name, an email address or a password to read a recipe.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

This site uses Google Analytics to see which recipes and pages are visited, in aggregate. Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits and does not collect a name, email address or other personal data from you. This site does not run advertising code, does not set advertising identifiers, and does not track you across other sites.

What this site does not collect

This site does not collect personal data through a form or an account. The contact form on this site is not connected to an inbox, and a message written there is not sent anywhere and is not saved.

Saved recipes

A recipe marked as saved is kept in your browser on this device, so it stays saved when you close the page and come back. It is not sent to Zaika Worlds.

Hosting and technical logs

Like most websites, the host may keep technical logs, such as the pages requested and the time of each request, to keep the site working and secure. This policy does not control the host's logs, and no personal data is sent to Zaika Worlds by this site.

If advertising is added later

Advertising is not in use on this site. If advertising is added later, this policy will be updated before it is used, and a consent platform will be required for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear on this page.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies for recipes or saving. Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits (see Analytics above). Saved recipes are kept in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie. If you play a recipe video, the video is loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may set its own cookies when you play a video; see YouTube's privacy policy for details.

Children

This site is for general readers. It does not ask for a name, email address or any personal information from anyone, including children. There is no account, login or comment system.
