Keema Naan

समय45 मिनट
सर्विंग4 naan
कठिनाईMedium
श्रेणीBreakfast · Pakistan

प्रकाशित 7 November 2026

Keema Naan is a lavish breakfast and celebratory meal across Lahore and Delhi. The yeast-leavened dough provides a crisp, chewy crust that encases a succulent interior of spiced minced meat, whole coriander, and chopped green chillies.

सामग्री और बनाने का तरीक़ा

सामग्री

  • All-purpose flour (maida) 3 cups
  • instant yeast 1 tsp
  • sugar 1 tsp
  • Warm milk 1/2 cup
  • plain yoghurt 3 tbsp
  • warm water as needed
  • Minced mutton or beef (keema) 400 g
  • thoroughly washed and drained dry
  • Onion 1 medium finely chopped and squeezed of excess water
  • Ginger-garlic paste 1 tbsp
  • green chillies 3 finely chopped
  • Crushed coriander seeds 1 tbsp
  • cumin powder 1 tsp
  • garam masala 1/2 tsp
  • Fresh mint and coriander leaves 3 tbsp finely chopped
  • salt to taste
  • Butter and sesame seeds / kalonji for topping

बनाने का तरीक़ा

  1. Prepare dough: Knead flour, yeast, sugar, salt, yoghurt, and warm milk into a soft, elastic dough. Cover and let rise in a warm spot for 1 hour until doubled.
  2. Prepare filling: In a bowl, mix minced meat, chopped onion, ginger-garlic paste, green chillies, crushed coriander, cumin, garam masala, mint, coriander, and salt until completely uniform.
  3. Divide dough and filling into 4 equal portions.
  4. Flatten a dough ball slightly, place a meat portion in the centre, bring the edges together to seal tightly, and gently roll out into an oval or round naan.
  5. Brush top with water and press sesame seeds and chopped coriander onto the surface.
  6. Cook on a smoking hot iron tawa, flip to cook the underside, or bake at 230°C (450°F) for 6-8 minutes until blistered and golden.
  7. Brush immediately with melted butter and serve with plain yoghurt raita.

Nutrition (approx. per serving)

490 calories · Protein 26.8 g · Fat 18.5 g · Saturated fat 8.2 g · Carbs 54.1 g · Sugar 2.8 g · Fiber 3.1 g

शेफ़ की टिप

Ensure the minced meat filling has no excess moisture so the dough does not tear during rolling.
