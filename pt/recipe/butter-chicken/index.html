Murgh Makhani (Butter Chicken)

Jump to Recipe
Tempo50 min
Porções4 pessoas
DificuldadeMédia
CategoriaPratos desi · India

Publicado em 7 November 2026

Invented in Delhi, Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani) is universally beloved for its silky contrast between smoky charred chicken pieces and the rich, velvety gravy sweetened gently by ripe tomatoes, cashew paste, and butter.

Ingredientes e método

Ingredientes

  • Boneless chicken thighs (cut into bite-sized pieces) 750 g
  • Yoghurt 1/2 cup
  • Kashmiri red chilli powder 2 tbsp
  • ginger-garlic paste 2 tbsp
  • Butter 4 tbsp
  • heavy cream 1/2 cup
  • Ripe tomatoes 6 large
  • pureed and strained
  • Cashew nuts 15
  • soaked and blended to a smooth paste
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) 1 tbsp
  • lightly crushed
  • Garam masala 1 tsp
  • honey or sugar 1 tsp
  • Salt to taste
  • oil 2 tbsp

Método

  1. Marinate chicken in yoghurt, 1 tbsp Kashmiri chilli, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic, and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Sear chicken in a hot skillet with 2 tbsp oil until nicely charred on all edges (about 8-10 minutes). Set aside.
  3. In a saucepan, melt 2 tbsp butter. Add remaining ginger-garlic paste and sauté for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in tomato puree and simmer on medium heat for 12-15 minutes until raw smell disappears and oil surfaces.
  5. Whisk in the smooth cashew paste and 1/2 cup water. Simmer 5 minutes until rich and creamy.
  6. Add the seared chicken pieces, remaining butter, garam masala, and honey. Cook for 6 minutes.
  7. Stir in heavy cream and crushed kasuri methi. Simmer on low for 2 minutes, then turn off heat.
  8. Serve warm with garlic butter naan or fragrant basmati rice.

Nutrition (approx. per serving)

610 calories · Protein 39.5 g · Fat 41.2 g · Saturated fat 19.8 g · Carbs 18.6 g · Sugar 8.4 g · Fiber 2.9 g

Dica do chef

Rubbing kasuri methi gently between your palms right before adding unlocks its fragrant herbal oils.
